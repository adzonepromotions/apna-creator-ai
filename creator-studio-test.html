4. AI Storyboard - 8 Scenes

Current MVP Limits

This build uses the procedural Rabbit and the rigged Rukh3D Elephant GLB with its included animation clips. Elephant actions are selected from each scene's Visual description (idle, walk, run, eating, sleep, wakeup, pick, drop, attack and trumpet where the story calls for them). During recording, mouth movement follows audio activity measured from the actual Stereo Mix input; it is approximate, not phoneme-synced. Export is WEBM, not MP4. Each scene includes a speaker label and Rabbit/Elephant use different voice pitch settings. Scene images and arbitrary new 3D models are not generated yet.
